Seismic research, connected.

SCOPED (Seismic Computational Platform for Empowering Discovery) brings open-source seismic software and research workflows to hybrid cloud and high-performance computing environments.

Mission

Supporting research across seismic data, cloud and HPC

SCOPED connects open-source seismic workflows and research computing to make data-intensive analysis and numerical modeling more accessible to the seismology community.

SCOPED Team

SCOPED is an NSF-funded project and part of the NSF CSSI program. The SCOPED project goal is to establish a novel hybrid cloud+HPC computational platform for use by the broader scientific community that combines large-scale processing and modeling of seismic data with a suite of open-source seismic codes.
